Free Online Coffee Cup Reading

Pick a topic, add a photo of your cup if you like, and a virtual reader will write you a short, playful fortune.

Entertainment only. Readings are random, your photo is not analyzed and never leaves your device.

How the reading works

CupSays is a playful take on an old tradition, usually called tasseography. You choose a topic such as love, work or travel, and a virtual reader prepares a short message made of symbols and sentences from our written library.

Every reading is assembled at random from thousands of combinations. It does not analyze your photo and it does not predict anything. Think of it as a small ritual to enjoy with your coffee, or a fun conversation starter with friends.

If you would like to try the real tradition at home, read our guide on how to prepare and read a cup of Turkish coffee, or explore the traditional meanings of cup symbols.

Frequently asked questions

What is coffee cup reading?

Coffee cup reading, also called tasseography, is a traditional kind of fortune telling in which shapes left by coffee grounds are interpreted as symbols. It is popular across the Middle East, the Caucasus, the Balkans and beyond, and is usually enjoyed as a social pastime.

Is this reading real or accurate?

No. CupSays is entertainment. Readings are put together randomly from a library of written sentences. The site does not analyze your photo, and there is no scientific evidence that coffee grounds can predict the future.

What happens to my photos?

Nothing leaves your device. Photos are only shown as previews in your browser, they are not uploaded to any server, and they disappear when you close or reload the page.

Is it free?

Yes. CupSays is free to use. The site may show advertising to help cover its costs.
